Title: Unsolved Burden
Date: 2026/09/05 10:49PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: existential, melancholic, reflective, religious

> - A burden was laid on me
> - I carried it some time
> - Soon it very wearied me
> - But I'd not like to whine
> - <br>
> - I pressed on in frustration
> - It made me tumble o're
> - Relief would bring elation
> - 'twould open many a door
> - <br>
> - For with this heavy load
> - I'd faint and fail beneath
> - My speed was surely slowed
> - I'd grit and clench my teeth
> - <br>
> - Sometimes the weight would lighten
> - Gratefully I'd sore
> - I'd walk and run and have some fun
> - Not long and returned my chore
> - <br>
> - I asked why this was so
> - And whether it could be not
> - Sincerely I wished to know
> - Why this had been my lot
> - <br>
> - Visitors came from near and far
> - To answer for my plea
> - They read the bones and the stars
> - Some costly, others free
> - <br>
> - Some would say that all was well
> - Others gave petty advice
> - Some told all that they could tell
> - I supposed it nice
> - <br>
> - One I knew had power alone
> - Whilst others proved contrary
> - To him I pled, a wretched groan
> - This one was not an ordinary
> - <br>
> - For one, I could not hear so well
> - His words were sparse and faint
> - I thought an answer he would tell
> - I thought not for constraint
> - <br>
> - Petitions born and passed away
> - Reply could not be found
> - But still I hoped for one bright day
> - When burden would be unwound
