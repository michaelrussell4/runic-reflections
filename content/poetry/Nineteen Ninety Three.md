Title: Nineteen Ninety Three
Date: 2026/09/24 10:53PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: existential, melancholic, philosophical, serious

> - It's good to know your way around
> - The fiery infernal pit
> - It's filled with such an awful sound
> - Death can silence it
> - <br>
> - I don't kill farm animals
> - But if I did
> - I'd use a BFG
> - I don't die quick deaths
> - But if I did
> - I'd be invincible, son
> - <br>
> - Two barons there await you
> - You think them you can beat?
> - In the end is darkness
> - No victory, just defeat
