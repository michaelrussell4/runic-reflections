Title: My Bird Must Fly Away
Date: 2026/09/05 10:17PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: melancholic, narrative, reflective, serious

> - A small and feeble bird one day
> - Fell into my hand
> - So helpless, innocent, and pure
> - But weak, she could not stand
> - <br>
> - I cared for her and gave her love
> - It was a fine delight
> - Her beauty soon began to shine
> - She was a ray of light
> - <br>
> - We played and laughed
> - We sang and danced
> - She learned so well
> - Her wisdom advanced
> - <br>
> - But somewhere back inside my mind
> - I had a sorrow born
> - For I knew someday she'd fly away
> - On some soon future morn
> - <br>
> - My precious little bird
> - I had to make her free
> - But how could I do such a thing?
> - She was my light, you see
> - <br>
> - She was my child
> - My daughter, mine
> - I loved her dearly so
> - Yet such would come a time
> - <br>
> - A tear rolls down my cheek today
> - Though still my bird's beside
> - She sleeps tonight all safe and warm
> - She will not know I cried
> - <br>
> - Someday my bird must go away
> - I'll watch her from apart
> - Her beauty, joy, smiles, laughs
> - Will fill another's heart
