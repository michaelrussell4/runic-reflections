Title: When I Close My Eyes
Date: 2026/09/03 03:32PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: existential, philosophical, reflective, serious

> - I close my eyes and those two there
> - Dark and black and eerie
> - Like two deep caves that at me stare
> - Stark and at me query
> - I'm drawn to them like gnat to light
> - Wisdom's well: I'll drink my delight
> - Echos of mystery
> - Arcane history
> - Recounts my life untold
> - Susurrous mumbles muffle faint
> - The sounds of mother's womb
> - Who are you? Who am I?
> - Silent as a statue
> - A natural answer: quietude
> - Peering there, being viewed
> - Time has wearied this creature's tongue
> - Yet unblinkingly owl eyes stare on
