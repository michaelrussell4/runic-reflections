Title: Response to a Tear
Date: 2026/08/12 09:18PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: romantic, reflective, serious, narrative

> - Patience and grace for my dear
> - Our race has thee tired I fear
> - Let us draw back our reins
> - Let us calm down our veins
> - In thine eye could there be a tear?
> - This dove is pure and light
> - Not made for this rigorous flight
> - She's laden with leaden load
> - Far our journey, long our road
> - In our abode we rest tonight
> - And when the sun bids us to rise
> - Our gaze must meet each other's eyes
> - Together we weather our journey through 
> - Whether the skies be gray or blue
> - I'll slow our pace, I'll stop and rest
> - For thou art my flower, my quest
