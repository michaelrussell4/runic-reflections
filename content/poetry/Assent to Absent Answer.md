Title: Assent to Absent Answer
Date: 2026/09/03 03:07PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: melancholic, existential, philosophical, romantic

> - Ignorance is not a lacking
> - Clear's not deprived of fog
> - As toxic gas fills to suffocate
> - As water drowns to retaliate
> - The tune to my melancholy slog
> - 
> - My malicious maiden blinds
> - She will not trust my sight
> - Hide from me
> - Run and flee
> - I pine for a glimpse of her light
> -
> - I tread, I swim, I gasp
> - I fall, I sink, a clasp
> - A rescue sweet
> - Past obsolete
> - Petitions are all now moot
