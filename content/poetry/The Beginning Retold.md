Title: The Beginning Retold
Date: 2026/09/25 09:13PM
Category: Poetry
Author: Mike Russell
Tags: religious, philosophical, existential, abstract

> - In the beginning there was darkness
> - And God swam upon the waters
> - He found an egg where he made his abode
> - In it he breathed his own life
> - From it sprung a man
> - His food was the fruit of the tree of life
> - A cord of life-blood delivering
> - He filled the measure of his creation
> - Indeed, the immensity of space
> - Through great pains he departed that sphere
> - Water breaking, blood spilling
> - Emerging forth from death to life
> - Carrying in him the seed of God
> - A new life, a new death, to reunite
> - From womb to womb, circle to circle
> - Life to life, universe to universe
> - Ends are beginnings and beginnings are ends

## Notes

The creation myth as told from the womb, recursively repeating as life gives life. We start from our own self-contained universes (wombs) only to die and enter a shared one—physical reality; then we die and reunite to our first beginning—spiritual realm.
